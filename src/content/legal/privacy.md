This site (alexandreroussel.com) is a personal professional portfolio. It is a static website: it sets no cookies, runs no advertising scripts, and collects no personal data from visitors.

#### Audience measurement (privacy-first analytics)

This site uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to measure aggregate traffic: page views, referring sites, device type, and visitors' country. It is designed to be privacy-first: it sets no cookies, stores no identifier in your browser, does not track you across sites, and does not share data with advertising networks. Visitor IP addresses are discarded at Cloudflare's edge and are not retained; they are used only to estimate the country of a visit at an aggregate level.

#### Information you provide voluntarily

If you contact me by email, I receive the information you choose to send (typically your name, email address, and message content). That information is used solely to respond to you and for professional purposes such as recruitment. It is never sold or shared with third parties.

#### Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As with virtually any website, basic technical data (IP address, user agent, timestamps) may be recorded in hosting-level logs managed by the provider. I do not access or exploit this data.

#### Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may request access to, rectification, or deletion of any personal information you have sent me. Contact: AlexandreR365@gmail.com.

#### Changes to this policy

If this site ever adds analytics or any form of tracking, this page will be updated before it goes live.
